- **Notes**: Only the WMA Pro decoder is enabled: `--disable-everything --enable-decoder=wmapro --disable-programs --disable-doc --disable-network --disable-autodetect`. No `--enable-gpl` and no `--enable-nonfree`. Linked statically into ArkeyanRuntime for XMA audio and into the kit’s Arkeyan-Builder for disc menu music (`apu/xma_decoder_ffmpeg.cpp`).   on their own machine, so no FFmpeg binary is conveyed by the project. The player has everything section 6 of the
