# XenosRecomp (Xenos shader translator): the patch, hunk by hunk

> **Purpose:** what does our XenosRecomp patch change, hunk by hunk, and why? · **Status:** current (2026-11-03) · **Tags:** [Xbox]

Upstream: `thirdparty/XenosRecomp` = **squidbus fork, commit 4906992** ("MSL shader support", dual HLSL/MSL, MIT), dxc from renderbag `dxc-bin` v1.8.2407. Ours: [`patches/xenosrecomp.patch`](../../../patches/xenosrecomp.patch)
(about 1790 lines; after a merge touching patches reset the submodule: `git -C thirdparty/XenosRecomp checkout -- .`). Behavioural summary and the ABI: [shaders.md](../../runtime/gpu/shaders.md).

**Rebuild the shader cache after any change to this patch or its flags** (`tools/shaders/build_cache.sh`; asserts stay on in the build: they reject bad shaders).

locations in the translator and in `d3d/shaders.cpp` `InputLocation`. Every target allows more: D3D12 32 elements, Metal
31 attributes, desktop Vulkan drivers 32 or more. A device under 18 would fail those pipelines [?].

## Numbers

SSA: 1118/1122 shaders translate and compile (4 skipped: 2 PSIZE0 VS before the fix, 2 shaders without a CTAB); Metal compiles cost ~0.5 s CPU each; the cache build takes ~1.5 min on an M1 Pro; DXIL 1939 containers, 20.6 MB (3.2 MB compressed), all accepted by `dxil.dll`.
